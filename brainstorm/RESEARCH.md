## 1. Direct Competitors — Sandwich & Lunch Ordering Platforms

These are the platforms most directly competing in the space Lunchbox would enter: helping sandwich shops (broodjeszaken) and food vendors take orders digitally, especially for group/office ordering.

### Smosser (smosser.be) — Closest Competitor

- **What**: Belgian sandwich ordering platform optimized for team/group ordering
- **Website**: smosser.be (consumer) / business.smosser.be (merchant portal)
- **Languages**: Dutch, French, English
- **Founded by**: Davy De Witte (Founder), Bram Van Geert (Business Developer), Pieter Bommelé (Front-end developer)

#### How It Works

1. **For companies**: Request a group setup via form; Smosser contacts you
2. **Share a unique link**: Distribute to colleagues for group participation
3. **Individual ordering**: Team members order individually before a deadline
4. **Automatic consolidation**: Orders are grouped and sent to the sandwich shop as one collective delivery

#### Key Features

- Individual ordering with automatic group consolidation
- Per-shift and per-location customization
- Flexible delivery (pickup or delivery to workplace)
- Summary emails at each order deadline
- No cash handling required
- Company invoicing options with optional employer contribution
- Parent/employee payment management (also targets schools)

#### Payment Methods

| Method | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Smosser Prepaid | Wallet system |
| Fast Checkout | SEPA bank account linkage with **EUR 0.15 discount** per order |
| Bancontact | Standard Belgian payment |
| iDEAL / Cartes Bancaires | For Dutch and French-speaking customers |
| Company invoicing | B2B billing |

#### Target Audiences

- **Companies**: Streamlined office lunch ordering
- **Schools**: Student ordering with parent-managed accounts
- **Individual customers**: Direct sandwich shop ordering

#### Merchant Portal

- Centralized dashboard for all orders
- Real-time sales performance tracking (daily/weekly metrics)
- Automated invoice generation
- Customer preference tracking
- Flexible scheduling for delivery/pickup windows
- **Daily payouts** (vendors highlight this as improvement over weekly cycles)
- Popular items tracking and active customer counts

#### Pricing

**Not publicly disclosed.** Merchants contact info@smosser.be for onboarding. Registration claimed to take less than 5 minutes.

#### Strengths (What Lunchbox Must Match or Beat)

- Purpose-built for the Belgian smos/broodjeszaak market
- Group ordering flow is well thought out (deadline-based, auto-consolidation)
- Trilingual (NL/FR/EN) — covers all of Belgium
- School market is a smart adjacent segment
- Daily payouts for merchants

#### Potential Weaknesses (Opportunities for Lunchbox)

- No custom domain support — vendors live on smosser.be, not their own brand
- No meal voucher integration visible (Edenred/Monizze/Pluxee)
- Pricing is opaque — may be a barrier for small shops
- No visible white-label/branding options for vendors
- Appears to be a marketplace model, not a "build your own shop" model

### Bakeronline (bakeronline.be) — Bakery & Sandwich Shops

- **What**: Online ordering platform for bakeries, patisseries, and chocolatiers
- **Scale**: 1,000+ bakeries across Belgium, Austria, France, Germany, and others
- **Website**: bakeronline.be

#### How It Works

1. Customer finds a bakery by zip code, city, or map
2. Browse full product catalog (bread, pastries, sandwiches)
3. Order and prepay online
4. Pick up in-store or get home delivery

#### Key Features

- 24/7 ordering from any device
- Allergen filtering (gluten, lactose, etc.)
- Advance ordering to prevent sold-out items
- Prepayment eliminates queuing

#### Payment Methods

- Visa, Mastercard, Bancontact
- **Ticket Restaurant meal vouchers** (2% transaction fee)

#### Business Model

- Transaction fee-based (percentage per order, exact rate not public)
- Free for customers

#### Relevance to Lunchbox

- Strong in bakery segment but not purpose-built for office/team ordering
- Has meal voucher integration (Lunchbox needs this)
- Vendors don't get custom domains — they get a bakeronline.be/shop-name URL
- No group ordering or B2B features

### EasyOrder (easyorderapp.com) — Branded App Builder

- **What**: Commission-free online ordering platform for food businesses
- **HQ**: Zaventem, Belgium
- **Scale**: 100+ locations in Belgium; clients include Belchicken (25+ locations), Takumi (50+ locations)

#### Key Features

- Custom-branded iOS/Android apps + responsive ordering webpages
- Loyalty programs, push notifications, discount codes
- Online reservations and table booking
- Inventory and time slot management
- Multi-store support
- POS integration via Deliverect
- Kitchen management via Apicbase

#### Pricing

| Plan | Cost | Target |
|------|------|--------|
| **Standard** | EUR 89/month per location | Building repeat customer base |
| **Professional** | EUR 149/month per location | Increasing repeat orders (most popular) |
| **Enterprise** | Custom pricing | 50+ outlets |

- **No commission fees** — fixed monthly subscription only
- No contracts, no hidden fees, unlimited orders

#### Relevance to Lunchbox

- Closest to "Shopify for food" in Belgium
- But EUR 89-149/month is steep for a small broodjeszaak
- Focuses on individual business branding, not group/team ordering
- No custom domain support mentioned
- Strong for chains, less suited for independent sandwich shops

### Pick&Go (pickandgo.be) — Frituren & Broodjeszaken

- **What**: Belgian ordering platform for chip shops (frituren) and sandwich shops
- **Apps**: iOS and Android

#### How It Works

1. Select restaurant on pickandgo.be or app
2. Choose from menu, select delivery options
3. Pay online (free) or cash

#### Key Features

- Queue avoidance ("Wees slim en vermijd de wachtrij!")
- Group ordering with friends, family, or colleagues
- Visa, Bancontact, and cash accepted

#### Target Market

- Primarily frituren (chip shops) and casual dining
- Some broodjeszaken participate

#### Relevance to Lunchbox

- Has group ordering but more casual/consumer-focused, not B2B office
- Strong in frituren segment, not specialized for sandwich/lunch delivery
- Pricing not disclosed

- **What**: Online ordering for fresh sandwiches and breakfast via Shop & Go stores
- **Locations**: Lokeren, Kemzeke, Sint-Niklaas, UZ Gent

- Order via app or website
- Pickup within ~20 minutes, or corporate delivery (order by 10 AM for noon delivery)

#### Relevance to Lunchbox

- Proves the concept of corporate sandwich delivery works
- Not a competitor at platform level

## 2. Major Food Delivery Players in Belgium

### Takeaway.com (Just Eat Takeaway.com)

- **Position**: Market leader in Belgium (~15% of desktop traffic in restaurant/delivery category)
- **Commission**: 8-12% for restaurants doing own delivery; 20-25% when using Takeaway logistics
- **Model**: Primarily a marketplace where restaurants handle their own delivery
- **Scale**: 85,000 food businesses registered across Northern Europe (including Belgium) as of 2023
- **Note**: Raised commissions by 1% across European markets in 2022

### Uber Eats

- **Position**: Second largest (~6.3% of desktop traffic)
- **Commission**: 30-35% (pays restaurants ~70% of order value; 75% for exclusive partnerships)
- **Fees**: ~EUR 5 delivery fee charged to customers
- **B2B offering**: "Uber Eats for Business" — corporate accounts with spending allowances, no signup/platform fee
- **Active in Belgium**: Since at least 2016

### Deliveroo

- **Position**: Third major player, UK-headquartered
- **Commission**: 25-35% depending on restaurant type (25% fast food, 30-35% fine dining)
- **Additional fees**: 1-3% order processing/transaction fees
- **B2B offering**: "Deliveroo for Business" — corporate catering with allowances, dedicated account managers
- **Other**: Has "Deliveroo Editions" (dark kitchen concept) and office catering service at deliveroo.be/en/cuisines/office-catering

### Real-World Impact on Vendors

- Master Frites (Brussels) increased turnover 30% via Uber Eats but loses 30% per commission; compensates by raising platform prices
- Restaurants commonly inflate online menu prices to offset commissions (confirmed by UGent academic research)
- Some restaurants strategically withdraw from platforms during peak hours to prioritize in-house customers

## 3. Belgian-Specific Lunch & Office Ordering Platforms

### B2B / Office Lunch Focused

#### Foodiz (Brussels)

- **What**: Belgium's first "digital canteen" for companies
- **Services**: Digital Canteen (mobile app for daily lunch ordering), Catering (meetings/events), F&B Counter (physical workplace counter)
- **Menu**: 6 restaurant brands (sandwiches, wraps, soups, bowls, bento, daily dishes)
- **Sustainability**: Reusable containers, local ingredients, rescued ingredients from other kitchens
- **Target**: Medium-to-large companies replacing traditional cafeterias
- **Built by**: Nightborn agency in Brussels
- **Pricing**: Not publicly disclosed
- **Website**: en.foodiz.be

#### Deliveroo for Business (Belgium)

- Corporate version of Deliveroo at deliveroo.be/en/business
- Employer sets allowances with rules (office-only, time-of-day restrictions)
- Employees order via regular Deliveroo app, charged to company account
- Use cases: team lunches, meetings, events, late-night working, snacks

#### Uber Eats for Business (Belgium)

- Free to set up, no platform fees
- Configurable spending allowances per meal/day/month
- Rules for expense codes, delivery locations, delivery times, alcohol restrictions

### Traditional Contract Caterers

- **Sodexo** and **Compass Group (Eurest)** together hold over 50% of the Belgian contract catering sector
- These are the incumbents for large corporate canteens (companies, hospitals, schools, universities)
- They operate on long-term facility management contracts rather than per-order platforms
- **Apetito Belgium** — provides prepared meals to companies, schools, and private individuals (apetito-food.be)

### Other Belgian Catering Services

| Name | Focus |
|------|-------|
| Le Pain Quotidien | Catering across all of Belgium |
| ChefMaison Belgium | Corporate events, office lunches, team-building |
| Belgocatering | Traditional catering service |

## 4. Multi-Tenant Webshop & Online Ordering Platforms

### Belgian-Focused

#### one2three (one2three.be)

- **What**: Belgian digital ordering platform specifically built for horeca (hospitality)
- **Target**: Sandwich shops (broodjeszaken), friteries, bakeries, restaurants, snack bars, ice cream shops
- **Features**: Online ordering (delivery & takeaway), QR code table ordering, kiosk ordering, kitchen display, POS integration, reservation system, capacity management
- **Payments**: Bancontact, PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, meal vouchers
- **Pricing**: "Pay only for services you actually use" — no commission on food delivery orders (major differentiator)
- **Languages**: Multiple supported
- **Partnership**: Growzer for horeca management
- **Presence**: Strong in Flanders
- **Website**: nl.one2three.be

### International Platforms (Active in/Relevant to Belgium)

#### Flipdish (Dublin, Ireland)

- White-label ordering platform — restaurants get their own branded website + native iOS/Android apps
- **Commission**: ~7% + EUR 0.50/order + 2.5% card processing (far lower than marketplace 25-35%)
- Monthly/annual subscription options available; custom pricing
- 15 countries including several European markets; Belgium not explicitly confirmed
- Raised EUR 40M from Tiger Global

#### Deliverect (Belgium-based, Ghent)

- NOT a consumer-facing webshop but a **middleware/SaaS platform**
- Aggregates orders from Deliveroo, Uber Eats, Glovo, etc. into restaurant POS systems
- 43,000+ restaurants, 300M orders processed, 42 markets, 400+ integrations
- Founded 2018 by Jan Hollez, Zhong Xu, Jelte Vrijhoef, Jerome Laredo
- Raised $65M (2021)
- Belgian success story but solves a different problem (order aggregation, not direct ordering)

#### CloudWaitress

- Commission-free online ordering system
- White-label, supports multiple languages including Dutch and French
- **Free plan available**
- Pickup, delivery, QR dine-in, table reservations
- Not Belgium-specific but could serve the market

#### Lightspeed Restaurant

- Canadian company that acquired Belgian POSIOS in 2014
- Full POS + online ordering + loyalty + reporting
- Strong Belgian heritage/connection

#### Zeew (Europe)

- White-label ordering platform for food, grocery, single-store businesses
- Multi-language, delivery apps included
- Claims 7-day launch capability

### B2B Supply Chain (Adjacent)

#### Choco (Berlin, active in Belgium)

- Free app for restaurants to order from suppliers
- AI-powered, automates order processing
- $1.2B+ goods traded through platform
- Active in Belgium, Germany, France, Spain, Austria, UK, US
- Solves restaurant-to-supplier ordering, not consumer-to-restaurant

#### APICBASE (Belgium)

- Food cost and kitchen management platform
- Targets multi-unit food businesses
- Part of Belgium's foodtech ecosystem

## 5. Belgian Regulations

### FAVV/AFSCA (Food Safety)

The Federal Agency for the Safety of the Food Chain oversees "farm to fork" safety. Three permit types:

| Permit Type | When Required | Process |
|------------|---------------|---------|
| **Registration** | Prepackaged, shelf-stable foods (no refrigeration needed) | Free administrative check |
| **Authorization (toelating)** | Non-prepackaged food sales to consumers (food trucks, restaurants, sandwich shops preparing food) | Involves inspection |
| **Approval (erkenning)** | Industrial food businesses | Strictest procedure |

- All food chain operators must be registered with FAVV **before** starting activities
- HACCP self-checking system is mandatory
- The "Food Hygiene Rating" system provides public transparency on inspection results

### BCE/KBO (Business Registration)

- Every business receives a unique 10-digit enterprise number from the Kruispuntbank van Ondernemingen / Banque-Carrefour des Entreprises
- Central database for all legal entities and self-employed persons in Belgium

### VAT for Food (Critical — Recent Changes)

| Rate | Applies To |
|------|-----------|
| **6% VAT** | Food products (groceries, unprocessed food, bakery products for breakfast) |
| **12% VAT** | Restaurant and catering services; **and as of 2026, ALSO takeaway meals and food deliveries of prepared meals** (previously 6%) |
| **21% VAT** | Standard rate; alcoholic beverages in restaurants |

**Key change**: Belgium announced a VAT increase from 6% to 12% on takeaway meals/food deliveries. Initially planned for 1 March 2026 but delayed after a critical opinion from the Council of State. Only prepared meals with shelf life up to 2 days are affected.

### B2B E-Invoicing (Peppol)

- Since 1 January 2026, structured electronic invoicing via Peppol is **mandatory** for all domestic B2B transactions between VAT-registered Belgian businesses
- Paper invoices and PDFs no longer accepted for B2B
- Grace period through 31 March 2026 (no sanctions if reasonable compliance efforts demonstrated)
- **This directly affects any food platform invoicing business clients**

### Platform Worker Regulations

- Belgium introduced a legal presumption of employment for platform workers (January 2023)
- From 1 January 2026, self-employed platform workers covered by compulsory accident insurance (cost borne by platforms)
- Brussels Labour Court ruled an Uber driver should be classified as an employee (June 2025)
- EU Platform Work Directive adds further regulatory pressure
- Historically, Deliveroo riders in Belgium worked through SMart (cooperative intermediary) — this arrangement ended

## 6. Payment Landscape

### Dominant Methods

| Method | Market Share / Preference |
|--------|--------------------------|
| **Bancontact** | 73% prefer it; 70% use regularly; 87.85% domestic payment share |
| **PayPal** | 55.3% market share (some sources); 39% prefer it |
| **Visa/Mastercard** | ~24.9% (Visa); mainly for higher-value purchases |
| **Debit cards** | 41% prefer |
| **QR code payments (Payconiq)** | 42% have used; especially popular ages 16-24 |

### Key Trends

- **Bancontact Pay** (rebranding from Payconiq by Bancontact, spring 2026) — 2.5 billion payments in 2024; 90% of online Bancontact payments made via smartphone
- **Wero** (European Payments Initiative) gradually replacing Payconiq by Bancontact for pan-European harmonization
- **BNPL** (Klarna, Riverty, AfterPay) growing in Belgium
- 82% prefer digital over cash
- 48% of Belgian consumers have abandoned a purchase due to complex checkout

### Meal Vouchers (Critical for B2B Lunch)

- Over **2 million** Belgian employees receive meal vouchers
- Providers: **Edenred**, **Monizze** (36,000+ merchants), **Pluxee** (formerly Sodexo Pass)
- Integrated into Payconiq by Bancontact app
- Accepted via Mollie, Adyen payment processors for webshops
- Meal voucher acceptance requires contracts with Edenred, Monizze, and/or Pluxee individually
- Payconiq allows combined meal voucher + regular payment in a single QR scan

## 8. Market Gaps & Opportunities

### Gap 1: Affordable Multi-Tenant Ordering Platform for Small Food Vendors

There is no dominant Belgian equivalent of "Shopify for food." one2three exists but is primarily focused on Flanders and horeca. Small broodjeszaken and traiteurs are digitally underserved — academic research confirms "local traiteurs and restaurants have not been forerunners in digitalization and are missing out on potential income." Many sandwich shops still take orders by phone or walk-in only.

### Gap 2: B2B Office Lunch Ordering for SMEs

Foodiz targets larger companies; Deliveroo/Uber for Business require minimum scale. There is no lightweight platform connecting local sandwich shops directly with nearby offices for daily lunch ordering. The space between "phone the broodjeszaak" and "use Deliveroo at 30% commission" is largely empty.

### Gap 3: Commission-Free Direct Ordering with Belgian Payment Integration

While Flipdish and CloudWaitress exist internationally, neither has strong Belgian market presence or native Bancontact/meal voucher integration. A platform combining white-label ordering with Bancontact Pay, meal vouchers (Edenred/Monizze/Pluxee), and low/no commission would fill a clear gap.

### Gap 4: Custom-Domain Branded Webshops for Food Vendors

No Belgian platform offers food vendors an easy way to launch their own branded online ordering website with a custom domain. one2three provides ordering but not truly white-label/custom-domain experiences. Flipdish does this but is not established in Belgium.

### Gap 5: Meal Voucher-First Ordering

With 2+ million Belgian employees receiving meal vouchers, and meal vouchers now integrated into the Bancontact/Payconiq app, there is an opportunity for a platform that makes meal voucher payment the primary/default checkout option for office lunch ordering.

### Gap 6: Rural and Smaller City Coverage

Major platforms focus on Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, and Leuven. Smaller cities and rural areas are significantly underserved for food delivery.

### Gap 7: Dietary & Specialty Niches

Vegan, gluten-free, halal, and other specialty dietary options are underrepresented on existing platforms, particularly for office/bulk ordering.

### Gap 8: Dark Kitchen Infrastructure

Dark kitchens are emerging in Belgium but with limited organized infrastructure or platform support compared to the UK or Netherlands.

## 9. Belgian Foodtech Ecosystem

- **178** food tech startups in Belgium; 50 funded; 1 unicorn (likely Deliverect)
- Notable names: Deliverect, Mealhero (smart steamer + frozen meal kits, EUR 3.5M raised), APICBASE, Paleo
- Belgian startups raised nearly EUR 500M in H1 2024; EUR 322M in H1 2025
- Key investors: Astanor Ventures (agrifood-focused), EIT Food (EU initiative)
- Belgium's compact geography (87.85% domestic payment share) makes it an ideal market for a focused national platform
